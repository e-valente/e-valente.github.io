On December 5, 2025, I helped organize the first iFood AI Security Meetup at our Campinas office. It came together quickly, without a budget: an idea among friends and colleagues from universities and the participating companies who wanted to get people in the same room to talk about AI security. Seeing researchers, students, and security practitioners share ideas throughout the day made the effort worth it.

The [full write-up on the iFood Security Blog](https://blog.ifoodsecurity.com/ai-security/ai/aisec/llm/ml/mlsec/2026/01/08/ai-security-meetup.html) introduces the speakers and includes recordings of the talks. They cover security in machine learning, attacks through MCP servers, anomaly detection at scale, and injection attacks against LLMs. If you couldn't make it to Campinas, you can watch the sessions there.

Update: The response was wonderful, and people still ask me on LinkedIn when we'll do it again. We don't have a confirmed date for the next edition, but we hope to hold one in 2027.
